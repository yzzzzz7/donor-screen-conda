Donor-screen report

This public chr22 example selects two donors for three F1 slots and simulates 50 F1 per pair. CDS annotations are synthetic. The short test fragment is used to demonstrate the workflow.

Run demo-two-donors · 3 simulated F1 offspring per plan · 6 recipients · 3 candidate donors

Heterozygosity and HBD are proportions in the tables and percentages in the plots. An em dash indicates an unavailable or inapplicable value. The control introduces zero donors.
