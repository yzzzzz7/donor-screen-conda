In the plan profile, heterozygosity, R and FROH are percentages; recent HBD is a proportion. An em dash indicates an unavailable or inapplicable value. The control introduces zero donors.

R is the mean across predicted offspring of the expected homozygous ALT missense count divided by the expected homozygous ALT CDS count. R is reported alongside the three-metric choice.
